# Aurora Cloud Storage - Backup and Disaster Recovery

## Automatic Backups
All files stored in Aurora are automatically replicated across three geographically
separate data centers in real time. There is no separate backup fee; replication is
included in every plan.

## Point-in-Time Recovery
Business and Enterprise customers can restore an entire workspace to any point in
the last 30 days using point-in-time recovery, accessible from the admin console
under Settings > Recovery.

## Version History
Individual file version history is kept for 90 days on the Business plan and
indefinitely on the Enterprise plan. Starter plan customers do not get version
history and should download local copies of important files.

## Disaster Recovery Objectives
Aurora's published Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours and Recovery Point
Objective (RPO) is 15 minutes for a full regional outage, as detailed in the
Enterprise Service Level Agreement.

## Exporting Your Data
Customers on any plan can request a full export of their workspace as a downloadable
ZIP archive at any time from Settings > Export Data. Exports for workspaces over
1 TB are prepared asynchronously and delivered via email link within 24 hours.
