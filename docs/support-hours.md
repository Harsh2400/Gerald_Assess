# Aurora Cloud Storage - Customer Support

## Support Channels
Aurora offers support via email, live chat, and phone, depending on plan tier.

## Starter Plan Support
Starter plan customers get email support only, with a target first response time of
2 business days. Support is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern
Time.

## Business Plan Support
Business plan customers get email and live chat support, with a target first
response time of 4 business hours. Support is available Monday through Friday, 7 AM
to 9 PM Eastern Time.

## Enterprise Plan Support
Enterprise plan customers get 24/7 phone, email, and live chat support with a
guaranteed first response time of 30 minutes for Severity 1 incidents, backed by a
formal Service Level Agreement (SLA). Enterprise customers are also assigned a
dedicated Customer Success Manager.

## Status Page
Real-time system status and incident history are published at
status.aurorastorage.example, independent of the support plan.
