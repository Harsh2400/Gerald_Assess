# Aurora Cloud Storage - Pricing Plans

Aurora Cloud Storage offers three subscription tiers billed monthly in USD.

## Starter Plan
The Starter plan costs $9 per month and includes 100 GB of storage, 2 TB of monthly
bandwidth, and support for up to 3 team members. It is intended for individuals and
small side projects.

## Business Plan
The Business plan costs $49 per month and includes 2 TB of storage, 10 TB of monthly
bandwidth, and up to 25 team members. Business plan customers also get access to
versioned file history going back 90 days.

## Enterprise Plan
The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and starts at $499 per month. It includes
unlimited storage, unlimited bandwidth, unlimited team members, single sign-on (SSO),
and a dedicated account manager. Enterprise customers can request a custom contract
with annual invoicing.

## Annual Discount
Any plan paid annually instead of monthly receives a 15% discount on the total price.

## Refund Policy
New subscriptions can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.
After 14 days, subscriptions are non-refundable but can be cancelled at any time to
stop future billing.
